# Lesson Plan Template

Use this template when the teacher requests a complete lesson plan. Write every heading, table cell and sentence in the language the teacher used in the request, keeping the English Bloom level names with a translation in brackets when the plan is not in English. Use a level-one heading for the plan title and level-two headings for the numbered sections. In guided mode, develop and confirm the relevant sections before presenting the final combined plan. Section numbers are part of the format: when an optional section is omitted, renumber the remaining sections consecutively so that the sequence never skips a number, for example 5 then 6 and never 5 then 7.
